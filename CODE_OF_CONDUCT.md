# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the BumpCompanion community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, appearance, ability, experience, or viewpoint. We will act in ways that support an open, welcoming, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes showing empathy and respect, accepting constructive feedback, taking responsibility for mistakes, and focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, sexualized language or attention, trolling, insults, personal or political attacks, publishing another person's private information without permission, and other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces and when someone is officially representing the project. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and participation that violate these standards, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when appropriate.

Use GitHub's built-in reporting tools for abusive content. For a project-specific conduct concern, open a repository issue containing no sensitive details and ask the maintainers to arrange a private reporting channel. Reports will be reviewed fairly, and the privacy and safety of reporters will be respected.
